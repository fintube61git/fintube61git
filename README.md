# T. Dawson Woodrum, PhD, JD

I am an interdisciplinary empirical researcher studying how constrained sociotechnical systems shape human agency, judgment, and behavior.

My work combines psychology and law with reproducible text analysis, structured human evaluation, blinded coding, reliability analysis, and privacy-aware research technology.

I am an Institute for Social Innovation Fellow at Fielding Graduate University.

[Research: Agency-Space Theory and Scholarly Invitations](https://www.tdawsonwoodrum.com/research.html)

## Current Research and Technical Focus

- Human evaluation, annotation rubrics, adjudication, and inter-rater reliability
- Reproducible R workflows using targets, renv, Quarto, and papaja
- Provenance-controlled corpus construction and sensitive-data governance
- Privacy-first browser applications using React, TypeScript, IndexedDB, schema validation, accessibility checks, and automated tests
- Guarded, optional local language-model reflection with user review and nonclinical boundaries

## Public Projects

- [IP Journal](https://ip-journal.live): structured first-person reflection workflow with browser-local storage and optional on-device AI reflection.
- [IP Mapper](https://ip-mapper.com): companion visual mapping tool that imports IP Journal files and renders deterministic relationship maps.
- [Clinical AI Evaluator Training Prototype](https://github.com/fintube61git/MH_AI_trainer): Streamlit prototype for training human evaluators to recognize safety failures in mental-health AI responses.
